05 · Responsive rules

What changes below 640px

  1. Header. One 56px bar: logo, avatar, menu button. The tab row moves into a side drawer with language toggle, email and sign out - no more half-cut tabs.
  2. Page header. Title and description stack full width. Action buttons sit in a 2-column row under them (grid 1fr 1fr), 44px tall, text on one line.
  3. Tables become cards. History rows turn into cards: week + teacher on top, status tag at the end, details as label/value pairs, row action as a full-width link at the bottom.
  4. Car cards wrap into 3 rows: car + transmission, teachers + Assign, then Edit/Delete as 44px icon buttons.
  5. Week grid keeps all 6 days on screen: short day letters, cells 48px tall with no "Open" text (the legend says it). Only "חסום" is labelled. No sideways scrolling.
  6. Publish moves to a sticky bottom bar, full width.
  7. Floor. Body text at least 13px, tap targets at least 44px, nothing nowrap except buttons and tags.
